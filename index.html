AI Exposure Explorer

Task-level exposure to generative AI for 427 occupations in the ISCO-08 classification. Browse the map, compare 2023 with 2025, or open an occupation to see every task and the reasoning behind its score. Switch the level to Poland 6-digit to see the 2,541 detailed occupations behind the ISCO-08 scores.

Scores run from 0 (the task cannot be automated with generative AI) to 1 (it could be fully automated). An occupation's score is the mean of its task scores; its category depends on that mean and on how much the tasks differ from one another.

Source: Gmyrek, Berg, Kamiński, Konopczyński, Ładna, Nafradi, Rosłaniec and Troszyński (2025), Generative AI and Jobs: A Refined Global Index of Occupational Exposure, ILO Working Paper 140. Data: github.com/pgmyrek/2025_GenAI_scores_ISCO08.
